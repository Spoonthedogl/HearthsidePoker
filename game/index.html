How to play

The goal: win chips by making the best five-card hand — or by being the last player left once everyone else has folded (dropped out).

Your cards: you get two cards only you can see. Five more are dealt face-up in the middle for everyone to share. Your hand is the best five cards you can build from those seven.

What beats what: a pair beats a lone high card; then two pair, three of a kind, a straight (five in a row), a flush (five of one suit), a full house, four of a kind, and a straight flush at the top. Open the Hand journal to see all ten ranked, with examples.

The blinds: before any cards are dealt, the two players to the dealer’s left put in forced bets — a smaller small blind and a larger big blind — so every hand has chips worth playing for. They shift one seat each hand, so everyone pays in turn.

How a hand plays out:

  1. Everyone is dealt their two cards and the blinds go in.
  2. Three shared cards are turned face-up (the flop), then a fourth (the turn), then a fifth (the river).
  3. After each of those, every player still in gets a chance to bet.

When it’s your turn:

  • Check — pass without betting (only when no one has bet yet).
  • Call — put in enough to match the current bet and stay in.
  • Raise — bet more; to stay in, everyone else must match it.
  • Fold — give up the hand. You risk nothing more, but you let go of whatever you already put in.

Showdown: if two or more players are left after the final bet, cards are revealed and the strongest hand wins the pot. Equal hands split it.

The chips are make-believe, so play freely. Stuck at any point? Open the Hand journal — it pauses the table and shows what you hold and what you could still make.
