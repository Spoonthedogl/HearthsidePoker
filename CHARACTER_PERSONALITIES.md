Each companion has three visual states:

- **Playing:** their usual attentive pose while participating in the hand.
- **All in:** a distinct pose after committing their remaining chips. Calling with their last chips counts too. This pose stays through the remaining cards and the result.
- **Folded:** a distinct resting pose after folding or while sitting out with an empty stash.

A new hand clears the previous all-in state. Companions with chips return to playing; anyone without chips continues resting. Starting a fresh table resets everyone.

## Conversation around the table

Remarks are occasional, with chances that vary by action. Checks are usually quiet; a raise or an all-in is more likely to inspire a comment. A greeting may come from one companion when the evening or a new hand begins.

Only one speech bubble appears at a time. A shared cooldown of approximately 6.5–11 seconds keeps the table from becoming chatty, and each character cycles through alternative lines before repeating one. Bubble durations and cooldowns pause while a guide or settings window is open, or while the app is hidden. Old remarks clear when a new hand or fresh table starts.

Dialogue responds only to public actions and announced results. It never reads hidden cards, predicts the deck, or supplies extra poker information.

Your wallet displays factual information such as a current bet, an all-in, or a fold. Otherwise, the small status area stays empty.
