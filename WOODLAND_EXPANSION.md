# Woodland table expansion

Open **Table setup** at the bottom right, or through Settings. Choose any size from **2 to 7 total players**, then select the exact guests. Starting a new table resets its chip record; cancelling preserves the current hand. All six companions are available at every table size. Sessions at every supported size save and resume with all private cards, deck order, pending actors and side pots intact.

| Companion | Personality and play | Hover reaction |
|---|---|---|
| Juniper, fox | Playful, small probing raises | Inquisitive tilt and light paw taps |
| Luna, moth | Observant and selective | Flutter and soft high note |
| Moss, frog | Unhurried, modest calls | Small nod and quiet croak |
| Clipper, crocodile | Patient; waits for value and bets firmly | Measured acknowledgement and low tap |
| Mur, axolotl | Curious about affordable draws | Little bounce and bubbles |
| Baron, dog | Steady caller, sparing raiser | Friendly wiggle and soft vocal sound |

Personalities adjust bounded willingness to call, raise frequency and wager size. They do not read other players' private cards or the shuffled deck. The existing limits on early re-raises and proactive early all-ins remain in effect. Playing styles follow character identity even when guests change seats. Each new character has playing, all-in and folded artwork. Luma is now Luna; old saved names migrate when resumed.

Hover reactions have a four-second cooldown per character and a shared sound cooldown. They also work with keyboard focus. Sounds start after taking a seat, respect mute and master volume, and use original synthesis. Reactions do not change the character's actual folded/all-in state. Modal/hidden-window pauses and Gentle motion remain respected.

The windowsill cat has a 1% chance of selecting a brief spinning dance whenever the scheduler chooses an animation. This is 1% of cat animation events, not 1% per frame. It is an original animation of the game's cat inspired by the [requested spinning-cat reference](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C43p8h99Cs0), accompanied by a quiet original synthesized trill. No video or soundtrack was downloaded. Gentle motion suppresses it. The bookshelf orb has a slow, faint internal shimmer; the right candle's flame has a slightly larger sway and height variation.

Validation includes 300 varied six-seat hands with chip conservation, six-seat save/restore, unequal-stack side pots, heads-up blind placement, restrained fair AI, roster-aware dialogue, and exact rare-animation probability boundaries. The native executable successfully created and resumed a six-seat table and preserved all 3,000 chips through a completed hand. Trusted pointer interactions triggered all six distinct hover animations and sounds.

The current seated artwork supersedes the original waist-up sheets and Clipper mask. See **SEATED_TABLE_UPDATE.md** for the latest visual and seating revision and **SEATED_ART_PROMPTS.md** for the complete built-in generation prompts. No purchased assets or external paid APIs were used.
