# Seated character art prompts

Built-in image-generation tool; original generated assets. Each file contains playing, all-in and folded frames. Real transparency is preserved.

## clipper

Create a production game sprite sheet on a genuinely TRANSPARENT alpha background (no painted checkerboard, no ground or scene). Wide 3:1 canvas, exactly THREE equal SQUARE cells in one horizontal row. In each cell show the SAME full-body seated character on the SAME simple small dark walnut wooden chair, feet and chair legs fully visible and uncropped, centered with generous transparent padding. Identical scale and chair position across three frames, each silhouette entirely within its own cell. View slightly elevated, three-quarter facing toward viewer's right, suitable for seating around a poker table; the whole chair and body must be visible, NOT a portrait. Crisp refined cozy pixel-art with warm amber light, fine clean pixel clusters, no outlines around rectangular cells, no text, no cards/table/background. Match a high-quality woodland cottage poker game. Character and 3 states left-to-right: a friendly olive-green crocodile with a long rounded snout, small round gold pince-nez glasses resting low on the SNOUT BELOW the eyes, no temples. Navy blue double-breasted tailored jacket, ivory shirt, copper cravat, charcoal trousers. NO cap, NO yellow cardigan. Patient confident personality. Playing: leans comfortably on one chair arm with one hand on knee; all-in: sits forward with one open palm extended; folded: reclines with crossed arms and amused smile. Clean finished edges and consistent anatomy, charming expressive different gestures. Transparent spaces between limbs, chair and around silhouettes.

Output: game/assets/clipper-seated-v3.png
